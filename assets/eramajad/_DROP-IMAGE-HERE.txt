Drop the HOUSE photo in this folder and name it exactly:

    eramajad.jpg

It becomes the full-size background of the "Eramaja paketid" section.
Recommended: a house / villa exterior, landscape, at least 2000px wide.
A light overlay is applied so the pricing cards stay readable.

To downscale before adding:
    sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 70 source.jpg --out assets/eramajad/eramajad.jpg
