Drop the APARTMENT photo in this folder and name it exactly:

    korterid.jpg

It becomes the full-size background of the "Korteri paketid" section.
Recommended: a bright apartment interior, landscape, at least 2000px wide.
A light overlay is applied so the pricing cards stay readable.

To downscale before adding:
    sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 70 source.jpg --out assets/korterid/korterid.jpg
