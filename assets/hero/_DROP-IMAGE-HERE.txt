Drop the HERO background image in this folder and name it exactly:

    hero.jpg

It will become the full-width background behind the headline.
Recommended: a wide, landscape-oriented interior or exterior shot,
at least 2000px wide. The page darkens the left side so the white
headline text stays readable.

To downscale before adding (keeps the repo small):
    sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 70 source.jpg --out assets/hero/hero.jpg
